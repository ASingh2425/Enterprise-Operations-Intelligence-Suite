# Business Problem Statement & Objectives

## 1. Executive Context
In global e-commerce and retail operations (such as Amazon Fulfillment Network), maintaining real-time visibility across complex supply chain networks is critical. Operations managers, logistics planners, category leads, and financial executives face fragmented data sources across ordering systems, warehouse management software (WMS), supplier scorecards, and transportation logistics.

## 2. Key Challenges & Inefficiencies
- **Inventory Mismatch & Stockout Risk**: Lack of centralized Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) and Safety Stock modeling leads to localized overstocking (tying up capital) while critical SKUs face stockouts.
- **Supplier Quality & Delivery SLAs**: Unidentified supplier defect spikes (above 1.5%) and delivery delays erode customer satisfaction and inflate fulfillment costs.
- **Logistics Latency & Freight Cost Volatility**: Carrier SLA breaches (late delivery > 5%) increase return rates and decrease Perfect Order Percentages.
- **Customer Churn & Segment Drift**: High-value "Champion" customers drifting into "At-Risk" status without automated early detection.

## 3. Core Business Objectives
- Build an **end-to-end Enterprise Operations Intelligence Suite** unifying 500,000+ transactions.
- Establish a single source of truth Star Schema model in SQL and Power BI.
- Provide automated predictive forecasting for 30/60/90/180-day horizons.
- Deliver automated natural language executive recommendations to optimize inventory by ~18% and streamline supplier replacement.
