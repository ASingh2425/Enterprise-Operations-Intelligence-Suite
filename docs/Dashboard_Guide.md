# Dashboard User Guide & Page Navigation Manual

## 1. Interactive Navigation Architecture
The Operations Intelligence Suite is designed as a 10-page single-pane executive decision suite.

### Page Overview
1. **Executive Dashboard**: High-level KPIs ($142.8M Net Revenue, $46.4M Profit, 94.8% Perfect Order Rate), rolling 12-month revenue trend, regional revenue donut chart.
2. **Sales Analytics**: Deep dive into category performance (Technology $54.8M leading), top SKU margins, and monthly order spikes.
3. **Supply Chain Analytics**: Warehouse capacity utilization (WH-US-WEST holding 580k units), supplier defect radar vs delivery SLA.
4. **Logistics Dashboard**: Carrier SLA comparison (Amazon Air 96.4% vs Regional Freight 86.2%) and route cost heatmaps.
5. **Demand Forecasting**: Interactive 30/60/90/180-day forecast visualization with Holt-Winters predictive band & 95% confidence intervals.
6. **Customer Intelligence**: RFM Customer Segmentation (Champions vs At-Risk) and LTV distribution.
7. **Profitability Analysis**: Waterfall chart tracking gross sales deductions (discounts, COGS, freight) down to net profit.
8. **Inventory Optimization**: ABC Pareto classification (Class A 80% revenue share) and Days of Inventory (DOI).
9. **Risk Monitoring**: Z-score anomaly heatmap for shipping cost spikes and warehouse stockout risk index.
10. **Executive AI Insights**: Automated natural language narrative card generator displaying actionable recommendations.

## 2. Interactive Features & Controls
- **Global Slicers**: Region (North America, Europe, APAC) and Date Period (2023-2025).
- **DAX Modal**: Click **DAX Measures** in top toolbar to open live DAX calculation catalog.
- **Filter Reset**: Reset button clears active slicer selections to default global state.
