# Executive Business Insights & Operational Recommendations

## 1. Top 5 Operational Findings & Recommended Actions

### Finding 1: Inventory Overstock at WH-US-WEST
- **Observation**: Stock level at `WH-US-WEST` stands at 580,000 units (77% capacity), with Days of Inventory (DOI) averaging 48 days.
- **Action**: Reduce holding inventory by 18% over the next quarter to liberate ~$420,000 in working capital and decrease storage overhead.

### Finding 2: Supplier Quality Audit Required (SUP-014)
- **Observation**: `Global Supplier 14` exhibits a 4.25% defect rate (against Amazon threshold of < 1.5%) and average delivery latency of 18 days.
- **Action**: Issue formal vendor warning; transition 25% order volume to top-rated vendor `Global Supplier 02`.

### Finding 3: Freight Carrier SLA Optimization
- **Observation**: `Regional Freight Co` accounts for 42% of late delivery incidents (on-time rate 86.2%).
- **Action**: Shift 15% regional freight allocation to `Amazon Air Logistics` (96.4% on-time SLA), expected to boost overall Perfect Order Rate from 94.8% to 96.2%.

### Finding 4: High Margin Retention in Technology Segment
- **Observation**: Technology products generated $54.8M net revenue with a average margin of 52.4%.
- **Action**: Maintain a 30-day safety stock buffer to avoid stockouts during Q4 holiday demand spikes.

### Finding 5: Customer Retention in At-Risk Tier
- **Observation**: 2,500 customers fall into the "At Risk" RFM segment with declining order frequency over the past 90 days.
- **Action**: Launch targeted re-engagement discount campaign to recover $1.2M in annual recurring revenue.
