# Key Performance Indicator (KPI) Definitions

| KPI Name | Formula | Target Benchmark | Business Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net Revenue | `Gross Revenue - Discounts` | > $140M / Year | Measures total top-line revenue after price promotions. |
| Gross Margin % | `(Profit / Net Revenue) * 100` | > 30.0% | Assesses gross profitability after direct COGS and shipping costs. |
| Perfect Order % | `(On-Time & Non-Returned Orders / Total Orders) * 100` | > 93.5% | Primary Amazon operational metric for fulfillment accuracy. |
| Return Rate % | `(Returned Orders / Total Orders) * 100` | < 5.0% | Evaluates product quality and customer dissatisfaction. |
| On-Time Delivery % | `(Orders Delivered <= Promised Date / Total Orders) * 100` | > 95.0% | Carrier SLA reliability standard. |
| Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) | `SQRT((2 * AnnualDemand * SetupCost) / HoldingCost)` | Optimized Batch | Minimizes total inventory holding and ordering costs. |
| Days of Inventory (DOI) | `(Inventory Value / Daily COGS)` | 30 - 45 Days | Indicates how many days current stock will support sales. |
| Supplier Risk Index | `(5 - Rating)*15 + DefectRate*1000 + DeliveryDays*1.5` | < 30.0 | Composite score rating vendor reliability and quality. |
